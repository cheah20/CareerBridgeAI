Prepare for your next interview and improve your skills with a step-by-step learning roadmap.

Interview Practice Questions

Practise these common interview questions and use the tips to prepare your answers.

1. Tell me about yourself.

This question helps employers understand your background, education and career interests.

Tip: Introduce your educational background, relevant skills and career goals. Keep your answer clear and concise.

2. What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Employers want to understand your abilities and willingness to improve.

Tip: Mention strengths related to the job. For weaknesses, explain what you are doing to improve.

3. Why do you want to work with our company?

This question checks your interest in the company and the position.

Tip: Research the company's background, products and values. Explain how the role matches your career goals.

4. How do you handle challenges or difficult tasks?

Employers want to know how you solve problems and manage difficult situations.

Tip: Use a real or fictional example. Explain the situation, the action you took and the result.

Your Learning Roadmap

Follow these weekly steps to improve your interview readiness and technical skills.

Week 1

Resume and Self-Introduction

Review your resume and prepare a short self-introduction.

  • Review your education and work experience.
  • Identify your strengths and skills.
  • Practise introducing yourself.
Week 2

Technical Skills Development

Improve the technical skills required for your target job.

  • Practise basic Python programming.
  • Complete a small SQL exercise.
  • Build a simple programming project.
Week 3

Interview Question Practice

Prepare answers for common interview questions.

  • Practise common HR interview questions.
  • Prepare examples using the STAR method.
  • Practise answering questions aloud.
Week 4

Mock Interview and Review

Prepare for a real interview through practice and feedback.

  • Complete a mock interview.
  • Review your answers and identify weaknesses.
  • Improve your communication and confidence.
